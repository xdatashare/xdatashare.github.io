The shared-data challenge
Sharing data between organisations often means giving up control. XDATASHARE resolves that dilemma.

Data value chains are held back by a lack of trust: who can access what, under which conditions and with which safeguards? Centralised solutions create dependency, silos and regulatory risk.

XDATASHARE provides the essential software components for creating a data space compliant with the specifications of the IDSA (International Data Spaces Association) and Gaia-X. It is based on the Eclipse Dataspace Components (EDC) connector, the open-source reference implementation.

The result is a data exchange in which every participant retains full sovereignty over its assets, defines its own policies and maintains trust without an intermediary controlling the data.

XDATASHARE is the result of Gradiant's research into data spaces: years of applied R&D transformed into production-ready technology.

How a data exchange works

Every data asset follows a policy-governed flow compliant with the Data Space Protocol. Push and Pull are two ways of delivering the same agreement.

1

Publishing

The provider defines an asset and its access and contract policies, then publishes it in the catalog (DCAT).

DCAT · Asset
2

Discovery

The consumer browses the catalog and finds the offers whose policies it satisfies.

Catalog Offer
3

Negotiation

The contract is negotiated through DSP. The policy engine evaluates the verifiable credentials.

Contract Negotiation
4

Agreement

Both parties sign a Contract Agreement that enables and governs the transfer.

Agreement
5

Transfer

The Data Plane delivers the asset over HTTP in Push or Pull mode, with real-time monitoring.

Solution architecture

One connector per participant, one governance authority

Each organisation deploys its own connector and portal. The Data Space Authority governs identities, participants and shared trust.

Participant

EDC Connector + Web Portal

Control Plane

Resource management, DSP contract negotiation with its own policy engine, and catalog and transfer control. The API is protected by an API key.

Data Plane

Secure HTTP transfer in Push mode, a proxy for Pull downloads, and monitoring of active transfers against the agreed policies.

Identity Hub

Management of verifiable credentials (W3C VC 2.0), identification through DID did:web, and credential exchange using the Eclipse DCP 1.0 standard.

Authority

Data Space Governance

Authority Portal

Participant onboarding, updates and removal; data space administration and monitoring; and use-case management.

Issuer Service + ParIS

Issuance of verifiable credentials and participant registration through the Participant Information Service, enabling discovery between connectors.

Logging + Vocabulary Hub

Activity traceability and publication of semantic elements — taxonomies, ontologies and vocabularies — in accordance with IDSA RAM 4.0.
